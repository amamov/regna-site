# Product messaging — 2026-10-25

Regna is positioned as a practical, free, on-device personal AI agent.
The user's direction excludes character-choice and friendship sales messaging.
The main promise is usefulness plus local processing and local data control.

Reference structure reviewed from https://www.pickle.com/ and its official Korean
App Store description: product benefit, concrete tasks, trust, install. The original
homepage also leads with friendship; Regna deliberately does not adopt that layer.
This is a design decision, not a claim that the original avoids character marketing.

Current page identifies development status. Requests are examples, not screenshots
or executed results. Phone calls/bookings/payments are explicitly not implemented.
No zero-risk or full-parity claim. No assertion that third-party services are offline.
